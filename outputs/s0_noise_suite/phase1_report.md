The remaining shortfall is **N2** (`char_delete`): its don't-empty-a-token guard drops a selection whenever every cluster of a one-cluster token is picked, and no implementation can reach the nominal fraction without violating that guard — so METHODOLOGY M1.3 reports N2's achieved fraction next to the nominal one. (N4 `char_transpose` previously fell ~22% short at severity 5 from its overlap-skip guard; the disjoint-pair rewrite now hits nominal exactly — `applied/requested` = 1.000.)
